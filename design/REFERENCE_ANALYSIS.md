## 2. Granular Reference Breakdown

### A. 5 Mobile Dashboards Study (`dashboards_study_0.jpg` — `5.jpg`)

#### 1. Big Big Box (`dashboards_study_1.jpg`)
* **Attractive Qualities**: One dominant visual focal point (countdown to next event), giving immediate temporal orientation.
* **Colour & Lighting**: High-contrast black environment `#000000` with pristine white typography and a calm amber/teal status indicator.
* **Typography**: Heavy geometric display numbers paired with ultra-clean sans-serif metadata.
* **Card & Depth**: Single elevated primary status card resting on an infinite dark background.
* **Relevance for Nimo**: **Today's Next Action** or **Current In-Progress Plan Item**. Users open the app and instantly see what matters next today.
* **What NOT to Copy**: F1 race-specific imagery or flashy sports graphics.

#### 2. Oura (`dashboards_study_2.jpg`)
* **Attractive Qualities**: Layers readiness, sleep, and activity around **one dominant daily score** before giving access to deeper analytics.
* **Colour & Depth**: Deep dark oceanic/slate environment with refined circular progress gauges and subtle ambient glows.
* **Typography**: Crisp serif/sans hybrid, tabular numerals for scores, soft muted labels.
* **Relevance for Nimo**: **Quiet Progress Section** (`Done Score` + `Self-Score`). Keep the scores dominant, side-by-side, without visual clutter.
* **What NOT to Copy**: Biometric health sensors, heart rate waveforms.

#### 3. Jomo (`dashboards_study_3.jpg`)
* **Attractive Qualities**: Radical simplicity. Pairs an emotional label ("Productive", "Peaceful", "Focused") with just enough metrics (percentage split bar).
* **Card Structure**: Soft pill tags, clean horizontal breakdown bar (e.g. 90% core, 10% bonus).
* **Relevance for Nimo**: **Daily Intention & Day Close Reflection**. When users close their day, pair their Done Score with an emotional state indicator.
* **What NOT to Copy**: Screen-time / app-blocking UI elements.

#### 4. Finch (`dashboards_study_4.jpg`)
* **Attractive Qualities**: Emotionally resonant feedback loop connected to a peaceful companion. Routine tracking that feels warm and personal.
* **Relevance for Nimo**: **Nimo Mascot (`NimoMark`)**. Nimo's sprout-hooded mark should feel present in the header and evening reflections, providing friendly grounding.
* **What NOT to Copy**: Gamified cartoon inventory, cartoon clothing items, or noisy RPG elements.

#### 5. Bevel (`dashboards_study_5.jpg`)
* **Attractive Qualities**: Extreme data density without feeling heavy. Compresses complex recovery signals into three high-priority rings.
* **Layout**: Multi-ring visual progress widgets with tight 4pt spacing and high typographic contrast.
* **Relevance for Nimo**: **Weekly Summary & Habit Streaks**. High-density compact views that communicate 7-day consistency at a glance.
* **What NOT to Copy**: Medical recovery indices.

### B. Drift Hydration Tracker (`drift_hydration_0.jpg` — `5.jpg`)

* **Visual Character**:
  * Deep night backdrop (`#0a0e0f`, `#020202`) with vibrant liquid teal/cyan luminous glows (`#00cddb`, `#38aff3`).
  * Detached floating bottom navigation and floating quick-add bubble.
* **Card Structure**:
  * Glassmorphic containers with thin illuminated rim lines and diffuse contact shadows.
* **Relevance for Nimo**:
  * Detached **Floating Capsule Navigation Bar** hovering above content.
  * Contextual **Quick Capture Orb (`+`)** with floating elevation.
* **What NOT to Copy**: Liquid water animations that distract from productivity.

---

### C. Subscription & Reminder Manager (`subscription_manager_0.webp` — `4.webp`)

* **Visual Character**:
  * Clean architectural minimalism on porcelain surfaces (`#F0F5F7`, `#E2E2E2`).
  * Crisp typography: serif greeting header ("Good Morning, Vikram") followed by quiet uppercase metadata.
  * Segmented monthly selector pill, category icons in soft tinted squares.
* **Relevance for Nimo**:
  * **Today Screen Header**: Editorial serif greeting + mono uppercase date chip.
  * **Projects & Workspaces**: Structured category cards with clear counts, active status pills, and deadline markers.
* **What NOT to Copy**: Credit card logos, bank synchronization flows.

---

### D. RiseUp Habit Tracker (`riseup_habits_0.webp` — `3.webp`)

* **Visual Character**:
  * 7-day dot progress timeline (`Sun Mon Tue Wed Thu Fri Sat`) with completion rings.
  * Dual toggle: `DAY | WEEKLY` segmented pill.
  * Clean metrics block: "4 of 7 goals completed (57%)".
* **Relevance for Nimo**:
  * **Today Screen 7-Day Strip (`_dailyProgress`)**: Clean dot indicators showing week-at-a-glance.
  * **Goals Screen**: Milestone progress bar with percentage tags and target dates.
* **What NOT to Copy**: Calorie counters or fitness trackers.

---

### E. Mobile Notes & Quick Capture (`screenshot_01.jpg`, `screenshot_02.jpg`)

* **Visual Character**:
  * Deep charcoal night palette (`#0C0F14`) with warm white text.
  * Distraction-free full-screen editor with subtle bottom toolbar.
  * Large conversational prompt: *"What's on your mind today?"*.
* **Relevance for Nimo**:
  * **Quick Add Action Sheet**: Direct, focused capture surface without modal friction.
  * **Items Screen (Notes)**: Quiet, elegant writing environment with generous line-height and editorial serif titles.
